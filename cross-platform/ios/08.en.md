# Apple’s App Store Requirements in China Mainland

> **Official policy verified. I have yet to file the iOS declarations myself.**

Apple’s requirements for the China mainland App Store were checked on 2026-11-25.

Apple announced the new **App Compliance** page in its 2026-11-16 App Store Connect release notes. Every app distributed on the China mainland App Store must complete two declarations, one for AI-Generated or Synthetic Content and one for Security Assessment. Answer Yes or No to each based on the features available to users in China mainland. Existing apps must complete both declarations and pass verification by **2027-01-31** to remain available on that storefront. [App Store Connect release notes](https://developer.apple.com/cn/help/app-store-connect/release-notes/) · [Apple’s China mainland compliance guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/view-china-mainland-compliance-information)

## What to submit for each declaration

| Declaration | Apple’s examples | Documents required when you select Yes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI-Generated or Synthetic Content | AI chatbots, image or video generation, voice or face generation and editing | Provide the approved filing number and registered name for the large language model (LLM) or algorithm. If you use third-party technology, include the provider’s authorization or usage agreement. |
| Security Assessment | Distribution of AI-generated or synthetic content, end-to-end encrypted instant messaging | Provide a screenshot from the National Internet Security Management Service Platform showing a passed assessment, plus a copy of that assessment report. The screenshot must show the app name, provider name, and passing status. The report must include the app name, provider name, and official company seal. |

Select No for a feature that the app does not offer or that is disabled or restricted for users in China mainland, as described in Apple’s guidance. Redact confidential and proprietary information before sending supporting documents. [Apple’s China mainland compliance guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/view-china-mainland-compliance-information)

## Declaration Verification and Availability

App Review approval alone leaves the app unavailable in China mainland while the declarations await verification. Release can proceed in the other selected countries and regions. [Apple’s China mainland compliance guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/view-china-mainland-compliance-information)
