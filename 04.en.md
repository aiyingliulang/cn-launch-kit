## Huawei AppGallery

- **Developer verification.** Individuals can use one of four methods: manual ID review, a personal bank card, facial verification, or Huawei Cloud authorization. Each individual can verify 1 account and no corporate bank account is required. Paid apps and merchant features such as in-app payments are for businesses. Individuals may publish free apps.
- **Copyright documentation.** Huawei’s qualification guidance marks software copyright documentation as **optional** and recommends obtaining it. It accepts one of three documents: a software copyright registration certificate, an electronic app copyright certification certificate, or a software copyright certification certificate. The name must match the app.
- **Privacy checks.** App Review checks personal information compliance. Before release, use AppGallery Connect cloud testing or the submission self-check. Common issues include obtaining device IDs or OAID before consent, omitting third-party SDKs from the policy, and preselecting consent.
- **Category qualifications.** Check the requirements against the app’s actual features. Consult the relevant rules for consultations, diagnosis, checkups, medication, or medical devices.

## Xiaomi App Store

- **Developer verification.** Individual applicants provide a developer name, legal name, ID number, ID scan or photo holding the ID, and phone number. The contact’s personal bank card is also used for identity verification. A corporate account is unnecessary.
- **Copyright documentation.** Xiaomi requires it **for every app at submission**. The official FAQ says a pending copyright application does not allow the app to go live first. Xiaomi accepts the same three types of documents listed for Huawei, including electronic copyright certification.
- **Privacy checks.** The Golden Standard Alliance, jointly established by OPPO, vivo, Xiaomi, and Honor, provides a permissions compliance check for sensitive permissions in uploaded packages. Xiaomi also reviews risky apps. Common issues include collection before consent, forced permissions, mismatches between the privacy policy and actual collection, and missing account deletion.
- **Category qualifications.** Match the app’s actual functions against Xiaomi’s special-industry list and the categories in the console. The exact category for the tracking tool still needs confirmation.

## OPPO Software Store

- **Developer verification.** The official entitlements table checked on 2026-11-25 lists themes, wallpapers, live wallpapers, video ringtones, ringtones, lock screen content (乐划锁屏), and short videos for individual developers. Android apps and games are business developer entitlements.
- **Submission plan.** Based on that table, this diary leaves OPPO Android submissions outside the individual developer plan for now.
- **Business submissions.** OPPO requires a software copyright or electronic copyright certificate, an app commitment letter, and an app filing. Its console offers privacy and security testing reports for uploaded builds.

## vivo App Store

- **Developer verification.** Official confirmation of individual eligibility for phone apps in China mainland is pending. The domestic onboarding materials focus on business documents, including a business license, the legal representative’s ID, and verification by corporate bank transfer or manual review. The global FAQ says individual developer account registration is currently unsupported.
- **Submission plan.** Check the entity types shown at dev.vivo.com.cn before preparing the submission. Proceed as an individual if that option is available. Otherwise, skip the channel or submit under a business entity.
- **Privacy and copyright.** vivo uses the Golden Standard Alliance permissions compliance review. Acceptance of electronic copyright certificates for domestic phone apps is unconfirmed. Quick App requirements have their own scope.

## Tencent MyApp

This section preserves the previous research. The current text on the official pages could not be retrieved during the publication check. Confirm the registration, copyright, and privacy page requirements against the official rules and console before submission.

- **Developer verification.** The earlier research found that mainland Chinese individuals aged 18 through 65 could register. Registration requires a QQ number, a second-generation ID card, and public-security facial verification. The individual registration route excludes overseas and Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan applicants.
- **Copyright documentation.** Each app submitted for review requires copyright documentation, using one of the same three types listed for Huawei. For electronic copyright certification, upload the original PDF and a screenshot of the copyright proof. For software copyrighted by another party, submit the authorization documents.
- **Privacy checks.** The official privacy compliance test uses a cloud device to check runtime behavior. It can be run before submission. The guidance reviewed specifies a standalone **standard static HTML** privacy policy URL. Blog posts, Weibo, GitHub, cloud notes, Word files, images, and embedded iframes are excluded.
- **Category qualifications.** Individuals can use ordinary utility categories. Check the rules separately for special categories such as medical services, which can require qualifications held by institutions.
